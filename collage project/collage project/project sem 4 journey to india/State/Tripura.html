Unakoti Rock Carvings

Unakoti, meaning "one less than a crore" in Bengali, is a site in Tripura, Northeast India, renowned for its ancient rock carvings, particularly those of Hindu deities like Shiva and Ganesha, dating back to the 7th-9th centuries. .

Ujjayanta Palace

Ujjayanta Palace is the state museum of the Indian state of Tripura and former royal palace of the princely state of Tripura. It was built by Maharaja Radha Kishore Manikya in 1901. It housed the State Legislative Assembly between 1973 and 2011.

Jampui Hill

It is situated about 220 Km. away from Agartala and is the highest hill range in the state bordering Mizoram. This permanent seat of eternal spring is situated at an altitude of 3000’ above sea level.

Jagannath Bari

Jagannath Mandir, located within the Ujjayanta Palace grounds in Agartala, Tripura, is a sacred Hindu temple dedicated to the revered deities Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra. This spiritual haven likely serves as a focal point for religious practices, cultural celebrations, and moments of reflection for devotees.
